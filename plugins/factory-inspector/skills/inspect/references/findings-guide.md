# Inspector findings: what they mean

Read this before explaining open findings to the user.

## Classes
- **deviation** — the factory did something its declared rulebook doesn't allow (wrong recipe, skipped step, approval for a document that wasn't presented, review on an unproven build, Claude doing something other than what `factory next` said, records written outside the CLI), or lost an outcome: a finding, a task's commits or an export that never reached what shipped.
- **ambiguity** — the rules left room and someone improvised (same situation routed differently, caps you keep resuming past, guard denials agents keep hitting, the same step repeating).
- **gap** — something the audit needs is missing or broken (no rulebook snapshot, a recipe edited without a version bump, a hook that failed and so enforced nothing, torn log lines, a CLI crash, agents that never stopped, a job left idle).

## Health rules
The health rules count what the factory reports about itself against limits in `.factory/inspect/config.json` (`limits`, `rules_off`). A **hook error** on the guard, Stop or prompt hook is high severity: while it failed, that hook allowed everything — say what could have slipped through in that window. For repeated guard denials, refused or look-alike replies, CLI failures, stale results, degraded steps, agent retries/failures/escalations, parks and resumes, explain the pattern and the likely cause from the evidence. For clarify parks (R19.clarify_parked), say whether agents failed (crashed, past their time box, the model) or a cap stopped work that was converging; for God Eye objections the user proceeded past (R19.proceeded_past_objections), say what God Eye objected to and that H1 showed it as an objection, not approval. For research gaps (R20.research_gaps), say what research could not settle and why (web off, no web access, no page for the confirmed version, a time box too small for the risk). Older jobs: for intent parks and dropped clarify questions, say whether the request or the judge looks at fault.

## Outcome rules
Work lost (R23.work_lost): name each task and the commit missing from the shipped head; the usual causes are a task rebuilt or re-verified after the last integration, a merge undone by reconcile's regression guard and never redone, or a job branch reset. Exports removed (R24.export_removed): name the export, its file, the task that added it and the one that removed it; a name still exported by another file the job changed counts as moved and is not reported, and tests are skipped; high means a later task's own range removed it, medium that no task did (an integration or fix commit). Ask whether the removal was intended — if so, the plan or an amendment should have said so; if not, it is a lost interface and needs a follow-up. Both are read from git and say nothing when a commit is gone. Drive stops never surfaced (R25.drive_stop_unsurfaced): `factory drive` stopped where the user had to act and no notification, inbox look, reply or message of theirs came after; say when it stopped and why (its kind), and suggest checking `policy.notify` and whether Claude showed the stop. A CLI failure count leaves out checks that ran and failed (a failing `task verify` is a verdict); the health summary lists those apart. *Parks* counts every park once, as R16 does.
