# Factory Console: what the page shows

Read this when the user asks what part of the console means or how to find something on it.

- **The floor.** Five tiles (your turn, running, parked, stopped, done) that are also filters, filters with counts on the left, and a filter box that takes words and tokens such as `is:waiting`, `risk:high`, `recipe:bug@low`, `gate:H2`, `has:finding`, `has:rework`, `agent:builder`, `since:2d`.
- **Board.** One row per job, grouped by attention: stage lights, the step or the gate it waits at and for how long, agents running now by model, tasks, return trips, retries, inspector findings and recent activity.
- **Line map.** Every job as a transit line on one time axis: stations per step, double rings for gates, dashed while waiting on the user, red return trips, parks, ends and follow-up transfers.
- **The wire.** Everything that happened across all jobs, newest first, including hook errors and refused "done" claims. Click a line to open that job at that moment.
- **A job.** *Line* is the flow canvas with replay: stages are columns, time runs downward, going back is a red line labelled with why, and each row shows the agents, results, findings, replies and guard blocks. *Story* tells the job in plain paragraphs and pins what it waits on with the exact reply to copy (e.g. `approve J-0004 H3`). *Tasks*, *Events* and *Lessons* too.
- **Findings and verdicts.** A blocker or major finding the finding-verifier could not settle (no result, or it answered *unverifiable*) stays **unverified and blocking** everywhere: the round's text counts it, the Story names it with the verifier's reason and evidence, and the step panel marks it *blocking*. Agent outcomes are coloured red when the agent did not do its job (fail, no_result, blocked, exhausted, error), amber when its answer holds the job up (revise, unverifiable, skipped_dependency), green only for a positive verdict (pass, done, approve, merged), and neutral otherwise.
- **Recipe map** and **Crew**: the whole factory for any recipe, and every agent with how often each ran and its Claude Code tools, read from the `tools:` line of its own `agents/*.md` file when the page is built.
- **Blueprint.** The same jobs as an engineering drawing set. *The line*: Intake, Contract, Design, Plan, Build, Prove, Challenge, Ship and Learn, each with the agents it calls on as a bank of lamps lit while one works (Clarify: the intent analysts, the judge and God Eye; Prove is a CLI check; Challenge: one reviewer per lane the jobs must pass, plus the finding verifier; Ship: the post-merge reviewers only when a recipe runs that review), red rails for work sent back to Build, side tracks for changing, parked and stopped (cancelled or abandoned, red ■) jobs, and numbered callouts for each decision waiting on the user. Then *Projection*, *Matrix*, *Schedule*, *Parts list* and *General notes* (lessons, accepted or promoted ones in use, the review rounds per job against the policy's `max_review_rounds`, and the project's own checks that prove runs). The title block replays the history and counts *Your turn* as the Board does (lessons and parked jobs apart). A job's *section view* shows the chat reply to copy and the same human-only command for their own terminal (`factory approve H3 --job J-0004`, `factory resume --job …`, `factory merged <commit> --job …`). Epics are not drawn there: sheet 1 names them and the Notes drawer lists them, with any epic gate waiting on the user.
- **Keys.** `j`/`k`, `Enter`, `Esc`, `/`, `⌘K` palette, `1`–`5`, `g w` (next job waiting for you), `g b` / `g l` / `g m` / `g c` / `g p` (board, line map, recipe map, crew, Blueprint), `?`. On the Blueprint, `/` finds a job (`Esc` clears it, a second `Esc` leaves the box), `Esc` closes a section view and the arrow keys switch sheets.

## Live mode
A served page (`factory console --serve`) updates by itself as the hooks and the CLI write to `.factory/`: the LED says **live**, only changed rows redraw, a job that moves group slides there, and a new gate brings a toast with the reply to copy (and a desktop notification if the user turns on the bell). An open job plays its new steps in if they are watching the latest moment and keeps their place if they are scrubbing back. A mounted Blueprint keeps its sheet, selection and replay place. A written file says **snapshot** instead.
